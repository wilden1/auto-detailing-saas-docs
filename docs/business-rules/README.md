# Business Rules

**Empty.** No business rule has been confirmed.

A business rule constrains behaviour independently of any single story — warranty voiding,
quotation validity, who may reopen a job, how a duplicate customer is resolved. Rules of this
kind exist in the trade, but **none has been told to us yet**, and none may be inferred.

Candidate rules currently live as questions in the
[open questions register](../product/open-questions.md) — see `OQ-021` through `OQ-028`.
When one is answered, it becomes a `BR-###` here.

## Adding a rule

1. Take the next free `BR-###`.
2. Copy [`templates/business-rule-template.md`](../../templates/business-rule-template.md).
3. **Record where the rule came from** — which person, on what date, said this is the rule.
   A rule with no source is an assumption wearing an ID.
4. Reference it from every story it constrains; add a row here.
